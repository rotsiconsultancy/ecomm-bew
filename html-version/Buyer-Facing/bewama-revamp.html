Procurement workflow

A buyer should always know the next best action.

Bewama needs a hybrid flow: quick checkout for stocked products and RFQ for bulk, custom, or uncertain requirements.

01

Find by search, category, or reorder

Large ecommerce winners make discovery immediate. Search should accept product names, categories, and procurement language.

02

Choose cart or quote from the product card

The page should not hide conversion until product detail. Stocked items get cart priority; bulk items get RFQ priority.

03

Confirm delivery, quality, and response expectations

Trust signals should be operational: availability, verified product, delivery planning, and quote turnaround.

High-conversion RFQ

Turn uncertain buying into a guided quote.

For industrial customers, the best CTA is often not just "Buy now." It is "Tell us the project, quantity, timing, and delivery point, then get a reliable response."

Modern ecommerce behaviors

Borrow what sells. Adapt what fits.

Search is a primary CTA

For large catalogs, search is not a utility hidden in the header. It is one of the most important buying actions.

RFQ deserves equal weight

Industrial buyers often need quantities, lead times, and alternatives. Quote flow should feel like buying, not a contact form.

Cart stays for simple orders

Do not force every buyer into RFQ. If a product is stocked and straightforward, let the cart complete the job quickly.
