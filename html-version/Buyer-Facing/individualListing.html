Product Overview

Somafix S50 is a high-performance, one-component acetoxy curing silicone sealant. Specifically engineered for professional industrial and construction applications, it provides excellent adhesion to non-porous surfaces like glass, aluminum, and ceramic tiles.

Its advanced formula ensures that the sealant remains flexible across a wide temperature range, making it ideal for expansion joints, glazing tasks, and general sealing in demanding environments. It is highly resistant to aging, shrinkage, cracking, and discoloration.

Typical Applications:

arrow_right Sealing expansion joints
