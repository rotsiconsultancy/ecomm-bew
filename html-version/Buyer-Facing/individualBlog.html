The Importance of High-Grade Silicones in Modern Construction

Alex Thompson

Technical Director • Oct 24, 2023

schedule 8 min read

In the rapidly evolving landscape of urban development, the integrity of structural seals has become more than just a matter of aesthetics—it is a critical factor in longevity and safety.

The Role of Silicone in Structural Integrity

Modern architectural designs often feature expansive glass facades and complex joints that require exceptional flexibility. High-grade silicones provide the necessary elasticity to accommodate thermal expansion and contraction without compromising the seal's weather-tight properties.

"The difference between standard sealant and high-grade industrial silicone can mean the difference between a 10-year and a 50-year maintenance cycle for large-scale commercial buildings."

Weather Resistance and Durability

High-grade silicones are engineered to withstand extreme UV radiation, temperature fluctuations, and moisture. Unlike traditional organic sealants, silicone retains its physical properties over decades of exposure to harsh environmental conditions.

Chemical Compatibility and Adhesion

One of the primary advantages of Somafix high-performance silicones is their superior adhesion to a wide variety of substrates, including anodized aluminum, stainless steel, and various types of glass. This versatility makes them the preferred choice for complex multi-material assemblies.
